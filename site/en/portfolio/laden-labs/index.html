Challenge

We built a public gateway for ethical hackers and bug hunters — a place where learning happens in process, with authorised practice as the premise.

Move

Three surfaces keep the same rhythm: a member forum for writeups and Oslo hangs, a challenge board with 24 JWT and SQLite labs from recon to broken auth, and the Laden Labs Console with fictional networks and lmap.

Stack

LLZ brings a live ops feed, forum, labs and gear into one dark, green surface. Gear uses NOK pricing and a demo cart with no real charge; Caleb stays in the loop as mentor.

Result

LLZ is live as a public gateway: Step on. Hunt ethically. People can practise with authorisation, share writeups, use the console and explore gear in one world — with the demo status clearly marked.
