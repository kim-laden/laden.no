Logger++ / Logger tab

Keep a full history of proxied traffic. When a bug reproduces once, your scrollback is the writeup backbone.

easy proxyburpwriteup
/burp/ · /tips/burp-logger/

Guide

Bugs love to reproduce once and then vanish behind a race, a cache, or a one-time token. If you were not logging, you are writing fiction.

Why it matters

Burp's Logger (or the Logger++ extension) keeps a chronological record of requests and responses. That trail becomes your PoC steps, timestamps, and evidence for a quality report — which is exactly what Lab Lounge trains in report-quality.

How-to

  • Enable Logger / install Logger++ and leave it running while you proxy.
  • Filter by host when the lab shares a browser with other tabs.
  • When something weird happens: pause, export the interesting rows, annotate in your notes.
  • For writeups, screenshot or copy the exact request that flipped the bug — not a cleaned-up rewrite from memory.
