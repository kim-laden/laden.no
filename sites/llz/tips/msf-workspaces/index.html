Workspaces keep you honest

workspace -a lab-oslo isolates hosts/loot per engagement. Don't mix client A with Friday CTF notes.

med metasploithygienenotes
/msfconsole/ · /tips/msf-workspaces/

Guide

Default workspace is a junk drawer. After three labs your hosts, creds, and loot bleed together and you cannot tell which note belongs to which engagement.

Why it matters

Workspaces isolate DB state: hosts, services, loot, notes. That is engagement hygiene — same instinct as separate git branches or separate report folders. Mixing client A with a CTF is how you accidentally paste the wrong credential into a ticket.

How-to

workspace -a lab-oslo
workspace              # list
workspace lab-oslo     # switch
db_nmap -sC -sV TARGET
hosts
services
loot
  • Name workspaces after the engagement, not the vulnerability.
  • Export/notes before you delete a workspace.
  • On shared MSF instances, never leave loot in default.
