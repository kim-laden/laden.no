Method

How the work actually unfolds.

The same offer, told in the order it happens. We listen, write the site down, and take a deposit before anything goes live. We build that thing and hand it over. Then a service cost keeps it up, every edit included, with no hidden costs and no tax on top. Set against the size of the project, the price is cost-effective. A lasting site and a time-limited one follow the same steps. For an event, or any other planned period, those dates go in the scope. If the page serves well, it can become permanent when you want that. After launch we ask for one sentence and permission to use your name. You can say no.

01

We listen

We start with you, not with a menu. What should get better, who uses it, and what sits outside the job. That becomes a written build: these pages, this form, this domain pointed, or whatever the work really is. You should recognise yourself in it. The scope is what makes the build cost predictable. Until we both agree, nothing starts. If the work is an event, or another stretch of time, the planned period is part of what we write down: when it goes up, and when it comes down. If it serves well, we can keep it and make it permanent when you want that.

02

A deposit, then it is real

Nothing goes live, and the build does not start, until the build cost or at least a deposit is in. A demo is for the meeting. The fee is for your site. From there the offer stays two prices: the build now, and the service cost later, if you want us to keep looking after it.

03

We build only that

We build what we wrote. If the job grows, that is a new conversation, not a quiet stretch of the same price. That is how the cost stays predictable, and cost-effective for the size of the project. Starter jobs in Oslo are often 15,000–40,000 kr. Below that, we call it a starter and cap the scope. You see the site working along the way, not as a reveal at the end.

04

It becomes yours

When it is done, it is yours. The site, the words, the brand pieces we made for it. The domain is pointed, the pages are live, and a short note tells you what you hold. The build ends here. The service cost is the next chapter, and only if you want us to stay.

05

We keep it up

Domain, the site kept up, a response if it is down, and every edit, change, and update you want. One service cost, and it includes everything. No hidden costs, and no tax on top. Yearly in advance is the natural choice. Monthly is fine, with a notice period. If the agreement ends, the site comes down. We say that once, here, so it never arrives as a surprise. The price is cost-effective set against the size of the project, and it stays the number we wrote down. A time-limited site or app gets the same care for the dates we agreed. All inclusive while it is up, and the same quality as a site meant to last. If it serves well, it can become a permanent page when you want that, and the care simply continues.

06

One sentence, with your name

When it is live, we ask for one sentence and permission to use your name. That line on the portfolio is how the next person recognises a real result. You can say no. If you say yes, it goes up with your name on it.
